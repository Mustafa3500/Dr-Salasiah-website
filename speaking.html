Signature talks

Intelligence That Creates Value.

AI is everywhere. But is it actually making a difference? From technology integration and proprietary data to sustainable business and intelligent cities, these talks explore where AI creates real value, where the hype falls short, and what leaders should be paying attention to.

01

Global AI: Beyond Adoption to Technology Integration

Everyone wants AI. But why are so many organisations still operating the old way?

Buying AI is easy. Making it work is another story. From ambitious pilot projects that never scale to disconnected systems and expensive technology investments, this keynote explores what it really takes to move AI from hype to implementation. Through technology integration, transfer and localisation, discover how organisations can maximise existing assets, strengthen capabilities and turn global innovation into measurable results.

ForCorporate leaders · Industry conferences · Government agencies · Technology summits

Formats
  • Keynote 45m
  • Fireside 30m
  • Executive session 90m
02

The Proprietary Data Advantage

Your competitors can buy the same AI. But can they replicate what your organisation knows?

As AI becomes accessible to everyone, the real competitive advantage may lie in something organisations already possess: their own data. From customer intelligence to operational know-how, this keynote explores why proprietary data is becoming a strategic asset, how AI can unlock its hidden value, and why owning information is not enough unless organisations know how to turn it into smarter decisions, greater efficiency and competitive advantage.

ForBusiness leaders · Enterprise executives · AI & Data conferences · Industry forums

Formats
  • Keynote 45m
  • Fireside 30m
  • Executive session 90m
03

Can AI Make Sustainability Profitable?

What if going greener could actually improve your bottom line?

For many organisations, ESG still means more reporting, more compliance and more costs. But what if AI could change that equation? From intelligent energy management to resource optimisation and predictive analytics, this keynote explores how organisations can turn sustainability challenges into commercial opportunities, reduce operational waste and discover where environmental responsibility and financial performance genuinely meet.

ForESG & Sustainability summits · Corporate leaders · Energy conferences · Manufacturing & Industry forums

Formats
  • Keynote 45m
  • Fireside 30m
  • Executive session 90m
04

Intelligent Cities, Sustainable Futures

Does a city become smart by buying more technology, or by making life better for its people?

More cameras, more sensors, more dashboards. But are our cities actually becoming smarter? From recurring floods and rising energy costs to ageing infrastructure and public safety, this keynote explores how AI, digital twins and integrated urban intelligence can help cities solve real problems. The conversation goes beyond impressive technology showcases to what truly matters: better public services, more resilient infrastructure and sustainable communities.

ForGovernment agencies · Smart City conferences · Local authorities · Infrastructure & Sustainability forums
